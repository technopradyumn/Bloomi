# 2. Product summary


What it is. A friendly health companion: track food and nutrients, water, steps, heart rate, sleep and body values, see local weather and air quality, and follow a simple daily plan. A cute mascot reacts to the user's day.


Who it is for. Adults (18+) in India and globally who want simple health tracking, especially people who eat Indian food and think in roti, katori and bowl, not only grams.


Core promises

- Log food by name or photo, with real household quantities (g, ml, katori, bowl, cup, glass, piece, roti, spoon).
- See calories, protein, carbs, fat, fibre, and later vitamins and minerals.
- Pull steps, heart rate, sleep, SpO2, weight and more from the phone and from any watch that syncs to Health Connect.
- Works offline. Backs up to the user's own Google Drive. Sign in once with Google.

Not in scope for v1: diagnosis, medication advice, social features, ads, any login other than Google.
